# Competitor Analysis — Payments Platforms

**Client:** Stripe (https://stripe.com)
**Audit date:** 2026-05-31
**Method:** Manual editorial review of public competitor sites + GEO engine signals.

## Key patterns across competitors

- **Developer-first messaging:** every player foregrounds APIs, docs, and integration speed.
- **Trust and scale signals:** logos, processed-volume figures, and uptime/compliance badges above the fold.
- **Polished motion and gradients:** clean type, generous whitespace, subtle animation.
- **Deep resource libraries:** long-form guides and docs that AI engines can cite.

## Where Stripe leads

- **Citability and schema:** strongest measured structured-data and citable-content footprint in the set.
- **Brand and UX cohesion:** the most consistent visual system and developer experience.
- **Documentation depth:** docs and guides are widely referenced, aiding GEO citability.

## Notes

The GEO column for Stripe (89) is the measured deterministic engine output and matches
GEO-AUDIT.json. Competitor GEO values and all Brand/Messaging/UX/Traffic figures are
illustrative editorial estimates chosen to show how the dashboard renders a leading
client against its peers. A real audit replaces estimates with measured values.
